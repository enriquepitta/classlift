# ClassLift · Logos basados en horarios

Exploración generada con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. Propuestas raster, sin cambios en la app. El usuario descartó los monogramas, símbolos abstractos y mascota de la primera ronda porque no comunicaban horarios de clases.

Archivos: 01-bloques-de-clase.png y 02-horario.png.

Revisión: nombre correcto en ambos. La grilla comunica mejor las materias distribuidas durante la semana; el reloj comunica organización del tiempo. Las variantes pequeñas son aproximaciones generadas y se unificarán en un vector maestro si se elige una dirección.

## Prompt 1

Use case: logo-brand. Original logo exploration for "ClassLift", a university CLASS TIMETABLE app. The user rejected abstract corporate monograms, abstract ribbons and cute mascots because they did not convey class schedules. THIS IS THE MAIN REQUIREMENT: the symbol should immediately evoke scheduled classes / timetable / periods of the school day. Create a professionally designed, visually compelling yet extremely simple flat logo, not an illustration and not a miniature detailed interface. Big bold simple filled shapes and intentional negative space, optical balance, clear at 32px. The image is a square presentation board, clean white background, generous whitespace. At top left small exact option label. Main hero: large original icon centered above the exact wordmark "ClassLift", spelled correctly in a warm modern geometric sans, medium-bold rather than ultra black, no italic. Bottom: two modest small samples of EXACT same symbol geometry, one circular social profile avatar and one solid single-color version. Show a finished, considered designer identity. No marketing slogans or paragraphs. No gradients, texture, grain, shadows, dimensional rendering, photography or mock phone hardware. Do not draw book, arrow, graduation cap, letters CL, mascot, faces, checkmarks, sparkle, rocket, paper plane. No borrowed existing app logos. Label exact "01 / BLOQUES DE CLASE". Design a clever minimalist weekly timetable emblem: a single softly rounded dark cobalt-blue square frame with THREE clear day columns, two small broad timetable-header tabs integrated at the upper edge, and ONLY FIVE large rounded rectangular class blocks arranged at different vertical times in the three columns. Use broad clean ivory negative-space gutters; class blocks intentionally different heights suggest duration. One accent periwinkle block contrasts with the deeper blue blocks. The mark should read as a class timetable, not a calculator, spreadsheet, building, bar chart, dotted calendar or tiny app screenshot. No tiny grid outlines, no tiny dates, no text inside the symbol. Strong compact ownable silhouette; contemporary editorial design and slightly softened corners, flat cobalt #3E51DB with pale periwinkle #A7AFFA and ivory. The large mark is predominantly blue and white, accent used sparingly. Wordmark dark indigo. The avatar reuses the mark within an ivory circle with comfortable padding; monochrome remains recognizable through the same five-block negative-space layout. Make it modern and approachable, academic organization without school clipart.
